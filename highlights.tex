\begin{highlights}
    \item Investigating the impact of outdoor surfaces on walking biomechanics is important better understand walking.  
    \item Data from lower back accelerations were investigated on seven different, commonly occurring outdoor surfaces in males and females.
    \item All surfaces---banked, sloped, and irregular---induce changes in non-linear gait dynamics, independent of sex
    \item Females exhibited a significantly higher short term Lyapunov Exponent than males, independent of surface type.
    \item Non-linear dynamics on different surface are needed in human movement analysis to fully capture the demands of outdoor walking.
\end{highlights}
